Services

What we help with

Exercise-based care for a wide range of conditions. Not sure if we can help? Ask us.

Pain and injury Chronic and lifestyle conditions Specialised care

Pain and injury

Back and neck pain

Posture, mobility and targeted strengthening to reduce pain and help prevent it returning.

Helps with: stiffness, limited movement, recurring pain.

Sports injuries

Rehabilitation and a guided return to sport, based on how you move.

Helps with: sprains, strains, overuse injuries.

Recovery after injury

A step-by-step return to strength, movement and confidence.

Helps with: weakness, limited movement, fear of re-injury.

Rehabilitation after surgery

Programmes in line with your surgeon's guidance.

Helps with: hip and knee replacements, ACL and shoulder repairs, spinal surgery.

Chronic and lifestyle conditions

Heart and lung health

Supervised exercise to build endurance and breathing capacity.

Helps with: recovery after cardiac events, respiratory conditions, low stamina.

Lifestyle conditions

Medically informed exercise programmes.

Helps with: type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, weight management, metabolic health.

Chronic fatigue and fibromyalgia

Paced programmes that respect your energy levels.

Helps with: CFS/ME, fibromyalgia, chronic pain.

Specialised care

Neurological conditions

Long-term programmes to support balance, strength and independence.

Helps with: stroke, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, balance disorders.

Women's health

Exercise before and after pregnancy for strength, core stability and a safe return to activity.

Helps with: pregnancy fitness, postnatal recovery, pelvic floor strength.

Hydrotherapy

Also called aquatherapy: exercise in warm water, which supports your body and reduces the load on your joints.

Helps with: arthritis, joint pain, early recovery after injury.

About hydrotherapy

Facility use

Independent use of our equipment for existing patients, under supervision.

Home exercise programmes

A written programme to follow at home. See fees.
