Services

What we help with

Exercise-based care for a wide range of conditions. Not sure if we can help? Ask us.

Pain and injury Chronic and lifestyle Specialised care
Pain and injury

Moving without pain

Exercise-based rehabilitation for pain, injuries and recovery after surgery.

Back and neck pain

Posture, mobility and targeted strengthening to reduce pain and help prevent it returning.

Helps with

  • Stiffness
  • Limited movement
  • Recurring pain

Sports injuries

Rehabilitation and a guided return to sport, based on how you move.

Helps with

  • Sprains
  • Strains
  • Overuse injuries

Recovery after injury

A step-by-step return to strength, movement and confidence.

Helps with

  • Weakness
  • Limited movement
  • Fear of re-injury

Rehabilitation after surgery

Programmes in line with your surgeon's guidance.

Helps with

  • Hip and knee replacements
  • ACL and shoulder repairs
  • Spinal surgery
Chronic and lifestyle conditions

Living well, long term

Supervised exercise to help manage long-term conditions and build everyday energy.

Heart and lung health

Supervised exercise to build endurance and breathing capacity.

Helps with

  • Recovery after cardiac events
  • Respiratory conditions
  • Low stamina

Lifestyle conditions

Medically informed exercise programmes.

Helps with

  • Type 2 diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Weight management
  • Metabolic health

Chronic fatigue and fibromyalgia

Paced programmes that respect your energy levels.

Helps with

  • CFS/ME
  • Fibromyalgia
  • Chronic pain
Specialised care

Care for specific needs

Long-term support for neurological conditions, pregnancy and exercise in water.

Neurological conditions

Long-term programmes to support balance, strength and independence.

Helps with

  • Stroke
  • Parkinson's disease
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Balance disorders

Women's health

Exercise before and after pregnancy for strength, core stability and a safe return to activity.

Helps with

  • Pregnancy fitness
  • Postnatal recovery
  • Pelvic floor strength

Hydrotherapy

Also called aquatherapy: exercise in warm water, which supports your body and reduces the load on your joints.

Helps with

  • Arthritis
  • Joint pain
  • Early recovery after injury
