Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how to ask for changes.

This policy explains, in plain English, what personal information Pétros Biokinetics collects through this website, why, who can see it, and how to ask for it to be changed or deleted.

In short

  • Forms collect only what we need to reply to you or arrange an appointment. No medical history.
  • Analytics cookies are only used if you click "Accept".
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Who we are

Pétros Biokinetics, 102 Dr. Libertine Amathila Avenue, Kramersdorf, Swakopmund, Namibia. Practice number 091 000 0880485. Contact: petrosbiokinetics@gmail.com or +264 81 683 5055.

What we collect on this website

Booking form

Your name, phone number, email, whether you have medical aid, your preferred date and time, and the general reason for your visit. You can also choose, if you want to, the area of the body where you feel discomfort (for example "lower back" or "left knee").

Contact form

Your name, phone number, email, what your message is about (for example "medical aid"), and your message.

We do not collect medical history, medication or other health details through this website. Please don't put them in a message. We go through your health history with you in person at your first visit.

Cookies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser.

  • Analytics cookies (only if you accept): if you click "Accept" on the cookie notice, we use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to count visits and see which pages are useful. This shares information about your visit (such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location) with Google. If you click "Decline", they are not loaded.
  • Your cookie choice: we remember whether you accepted or declined in your browser, so we don't ask again.
  • Small conveniences: the website may remember small things in your browser, such as that you have already seen the "drag to rotate" hint on the 3D spine. This stays on your device.

You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page, or by clearing your browser's cookies.

Other services on this site

  • WhatsApp and call buttons: the green WhatsApp button and the call button only open WhatsApp or your phone. Nothing is sent until you choose to send a message or make the call. Messages on WhatsApp are covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Map: the map and the "Get directions" button on our Contact page are provided by Google Maps. Google may collect information when you use them.
  • Fonts and images: our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and some photos from Cloudinary, an image service. Like any website, these services see your device's internet address when they send the files.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your message and to arrange or confirm your appointment.
  • To understand how the website is used, if you allow analytics.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Form messages are sent to us by email through Formspree, a form service, and arrive in the practice email inbox. Only the practitioner and practice staff read them. Formspree and Google process the data on our behalf and may store it outside Namibia.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and contact messages only as long as we need them to arrange your care or answer your question. If you become a patient, your clinical records are kept separately, as required for healthcare records.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it (unless we must keep it by law). Email petrosbiokinetics@gmail.com or call +264 81 683 5055.
