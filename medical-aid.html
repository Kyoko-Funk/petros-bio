Medical aid

Medical aid explained

Most of our patients use their medical aid. Here is how it works with us.

Start here

Which medical aid do you have?

Choose yours to see how paying works.

We claim directly

Your scheme pays us directly. We submit the claims for you, so you don't pay upfront for sessions covered by your benefit.

  • We check your available benefit before you start.
  • Most schemes need a referral from your doctor (see below).

Pay and claim back

With your medical aid, you pay us and claim the amount back yourself.

  • We give you an itemised invoice with treatment dates, tariff codes and our practice number (091 000 0880485) to submit.
  • Ask your medical aid what your plan covers before you start.

Private client

You pay our private fees by EFT. Monthly packages are usually the best value.

Your benefit

How biokinetics benefits work

On most plans, biokinetics falls under an "auxiliary services" benefit: a yearly family limit shared with physiotherapy, dieticians, chiropractors, psychologists and similar services.

Example: one family's yearly auxiliary benefit

  • Biokinetics
  • Physiotherapy
  • Other family members
  • Still available

An illustration only. If you or a family member see a physiotherapist or dietician, it comes off the same amount.

  1. Before you start

    We check how much benefit you have left.

  2. Each session

    We claim it from your medical aid at the official tariff.

  3. Running low

    We tell you in advance when it is close to running out.

  4. Used up

    Pause until it renews in January, or continue at our private rate.

Do I need a referral?

You can book without one. If you want to use your medical aid, most schemes need a referral from a doctor or other registered practitioner before they will pay for biokinetics.

Please bring the referral, or your doctor's name and practice number, to your first visit. Not sure? Ask us and we'll help.

What to bring

  • Your medical aid card (and the main member's details, if you are a dependant)
  • Your ID
  • Your referral, and any recent reports or scans

Claims and statements

We submit claims to your medical aid on your behalf.

Claims must reach the medical aid in time (for NHP, within 4 months), so please give us your correct details from the start.

If a claim is rejected, we let you know and help sort it out with the medical aid.

For doctors and therapists

Referring a patient

We work alongside GPs, specialists, surgeons and physiotherapists. Referring is simple, and you hear back from us.

  1. 1

    Give your patient a referral

    Your own letterhead is fine. Helpful to include: the diagnosis or reason, relevant history, any precautions or restrictions, and the surgery date after an operation.

  2. 2

    Your patient books with us

    By phone, WhatsApp or online. Most patients are seen within a few days.

  3. 3

    You receive a report

    After the assessment, Jean-Pierre sends you his findings and the planned programme, with updates after re-evaluations.
