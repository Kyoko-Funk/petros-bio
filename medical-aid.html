Medical aid

Medical aid explained

Most of our patients use their medical aid. Here is how it works with us.

Schemes we claim from directly

  • Namibia Health Plan (NHP)
  • Namibia Medical Care (NMC), administered by Methealth
  • NAMMED, administered by Medscheme
  • Renaissance Health Medical Aid Fund (RMA), administered by Prosperity

Pay and claim back

PSEMAS

You pay us upfront and claim the amount back from PSEMAS. We give you a detailed invoice to submit.

International and other medical aids

You pay us and claim back from your medical aid. We provide an itemised invoice with treatment dates, tariff codes and our practice number (091 000 0880485).

Your benefit

How biokinetics benefits work

  • On most plans, biokinetics falls under an "auxiliary services" benefit. This is usually a yearly family limit shared with physiotherapy, dieticians, chiropractors, psychologists and similar services. If you or a family member see one of those, it comes off the same amount.
  • Each session is claimed at the official tariff.
  • We check your available benefit before you start and keep an eye on it as you go. We'll tell you in advance when it is close to running out.
  • When your benefit is used up, you can pause until it renews in January, or continue at our private rate (see Fees).

Do I need a referral?

Medical aids usually require a referral from a doctor or other registered practitioner before they will pay for biokinetics. Please bring the referral, or the doctor's name and practice number, to your first visit.

What to bring to your first visit

  • Your medical aid card (and the main member's details, if you are a dependant)
  • Your ID
  • Your referral, and any recent reports or scans

Claims and statements

  • We submit claims to your medical aid on your behalf.
  • Claims must reach the medical aid within a set time (for NHP, 4 months after treatment), so please give us your correct medical aid details from the start.
  • If a claim is rejected, we will let you know and help sort it out with the medical aid.

Questions about your cover? We are happy to help.
