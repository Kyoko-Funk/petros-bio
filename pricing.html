Good to know

  • Monthly fees are payable in advance, at the beginning of each month.
  • Monthly fees reserve your regular session times. The fee stays the same if sessions are missed during the month.
  • If you start part-way through a month, the first month is charged pro rata.
  • Cancellations: a full cancellation fee applies if an appointment is not kept, or is cancelled less than 12 hours beforehand.
  • Pétros Biokinetics does not charge VAT, so invoices show no VAT.
