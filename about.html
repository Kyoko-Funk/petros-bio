Your biokineticist

Jean‑Pierre Grobler

Jean-Pierre qualified as a biokineticist at the University of Johannesburg in 2018. What he enjoys most is helping people reach their goals, whether that is walking without pain, getting back to sport or managing a long-term condition.

He is patient and takes the time to explain every step, so you always know why you are doing an exercise. Sessions are relaxed, and there is always some laughter in the practice, often thanks to his two pugs.

What to expect from us

Our principles

Movement is medicine. Recovery is a journey, not a destination.

Patient-centred

Your goals guide every decision. We listen, adapt and support you along the way.

Every step explained

You will always know what you are doing and why, so you can keep going with confidence.

Results you can measure

Evidence-based exercise, with your movement and strength re-tested to show your progress.

Meet the team

Peanut and Lily

Jean-Pierre's two pugs are part of the practice. Lily is the slim, graceful one, always ready for a stretch. Peanut is a little rounder and takes a more relaxed approach to exercise.

They are usually the first to say hello, and often the reason for a smile during a hard session.

Not a dog person, or allergic? Just let us know before your visit.
