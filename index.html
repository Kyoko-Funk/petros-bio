What is biokinetics?

Exercise as therapy

Biokinetics uses structured, supervised exercise to improve your health and how your body works. We assess how you move, then build a programme to help you recover from injury or surgery, manage long-term conditions, and get back to what you enjoy.

βίος + κίνησις = life through movement
