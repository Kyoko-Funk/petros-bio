What is biokinetics?

Exercise as therapy

βίος + κίνησις = life through movement

Biokinetics is exercise-based therapy that uses structured, supervised movement to improve your health and physical function. A biokineticist assesses how your body moves and builds a programme to help you recover from injury or surgery, manage long-term conditions such as diabetes or high blood pressure, and get back to the activities you enjoy.

It is often the final phase of rehabilitation: bridging the gap between treatment and full, confident movement.
