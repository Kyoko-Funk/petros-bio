Our method

How we work

Every programme follows the same three steps, so you always know where you are and what comes next.

It starts with how you move

Step 1 · Assessment

Understanding how you move

Your first visit is a full evaluation. Depending on your needs this may include an ergological assessment (strength, endurance and function), a functional work assessment, and a body composition analysis.

We also discuss your history, goals, and any reports from your doctor or physiotherapist.

Step 2 · Prescription

Your personalised plan

We design an exercise programme around your condition, goals and current ability, including what to do in sessions and, where helpful, at home.

Step 3 · Guided progress

Progress you can see

Sessions are supervised and adjusted as you improve. Regular re-evaluations measure your progress against your starting point, so changes are based on results.

Why this works

Simple, supervised, measured

  • Exercise prescription based on current evidence
  • Supervision, so exercises are done safely and correctly
  • Programmes that adapt as you progress
  • Progress measured at re-evaluations
