Your first visit

It takes 30 to 60 minutes. You will complete a short health questionnaire at your first visit.

Walk-ins are welcome; we will usually give you a booking for your assessment.

Wear and bring

  • Comfortable exercise clothes and trainers
  • Your medical aid card and ID
  • Your referral, if you have one (do I need one?)
  • Any recent reports or scans (X-rays, MRI)
